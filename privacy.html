Lamp and Path Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026

Lamp and Path is published by Michael Emery. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: what you say, write or photograph stays on your iPhone. There is no account, and we run no server that holds your data.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Listening. When you start a listening session, your speech is turned into text on your iPhone using Apple's on-device speech recognition. The audio is not saved and is discarded when the session ends. The app listens only while it is open on screen and only after you start a session.
  • Screenshots and photos. Text is read from the image on your iPhone. The image itself is not kept.
  • Writing. Anything you type or paste is stored only on your iPhone.
  • Understanding what you shared. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, the app uses Apple's on-device model to understand your situation and write a few lines and a short prayer about it. This runs on your iPhone; nothing is sent to Apple or to us. Anything written this way is marked "Written by Apple Intelligence on this iPhone". The summary it writes is deleted along with your words.
  • Moments, themes, reflections and plans. These are stored in an encrypted file on your iPhone (AES-256, using Apple's built-in encryption). The encryption key is held in the iPhone's Keychain and never leaves the device.
  • Your first name, if you choose to give it, and your settings.

We cannot see any of this. If your iPhone backup includes app data, the backed-up copy stays encrypted, and the key cannot be restored to any other device.

What leaves your iPhone

  • Subscription purchases. If you subscribe to Lamp and Path Plus, Apple handles the payment. We never see your card details. To confirm your subscription, the app uses a service called RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app identifier, your purchase history for this app, and basic technical details such as your country, device model and IP address. This is not linked to your name and is not used to track you across other apps or websites. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.
  • Links you open. If you tap a link, for example to a support service or to these pages, it opens in your browser and that website's own policy applies.

The app contains no advertising, no analytics SDK and no third-party trackers. RevenueCat gives us aggregate subscription statistics (for example, how many people started a trial), which never include anything you shared in the app.

Subscription data: your rights

  • Who is responsible. Michael Emery, the publisher of Lamp and Path, is responsible for this data. RevenueCat, Inc. processes it on our behalf under a data processing agreement.
  • Why we may use it. To provide the subscription you asked for (performance of a contract) and to keep basic, aggregate records of how subscriptions are going (our legitimate interest in running the app).
  • Where it goes. RevenueCat stores data in the United States. Transfers from the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (and the UK addendum) in RevenueCat's data processing agreement.
  • How long it is kept. For as long as your subscription is active and then for as long as needed for tax, accounting and legal records, after which it is deleted.
  • Your rights. Depending on where you live, you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the use of this data, or ask for a copy of it. Because it is tied only to an anonymous app identifier, include the approximate date you subscribed so we can find it. Email us at the address below. We acknowledge every request or complaint within 30 days and answer within one month.
  • Complaints. You can also complain to your local data protection authority, for example the UK Information Commissioner's Office, the authority in your EU country, or the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent from a server. Notification text never names a theme or quotes what you shared.

Safety

If what you share suggests you may be in danger, the app shows support services for your region instead of scripture. This check happens on your iPhone. Nobody is notified.

Your control

  • See everything the app holds, in You → What Lamp and Path holds.
  • Correct or delete any Moment, or forget all themes.
  • Choose how long Moments are kept.
  • Export your data as a file.
  • Erase everything. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone.

We hold no copy of anything you share in the app, so only you can see, correct or delete it, using the controls above. For subscription data held by RevenueCat, see "Subscription data: your rights". To manage or cancel a subscription, use your Apple ID settings.

Children

Lamp and Path is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.
