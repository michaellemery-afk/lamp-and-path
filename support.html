Lamp and Path Support

Email hello@atlasandguide.com. We aim to reply within two working days.

Lamp and Path is not a crisis service. If you are in danger, call your local emergency number. You can find free, confidential help lines for your country at findahelpline.com.

Listening does not start

Open iPhone Settings, find Lamp and Path, and turn on Microphone and Speech Recognition. Listening needs on-device speech recognition, which is available when your iPhone language is English. If it is not available, you can write or share a screenshot instead.

Listening paused by itself

The app listens only while it is open on screen. If you switch apps or lock the phone, it pauses. A session also ends after 30 minutes.

I am not getting notifications

Open iPhone Settings, find Lamp and Path, and allow Notifications. Then check your times in You → Schedules, including quiet hours and the daily limit.

Cancel or change my subscription

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Apple handles all billing and refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Restore a purchase

Open the Lamp and Path Plus screen and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

I have a new iPhone

Your Moments are stored only on the old iPhone and are encrypted with a key that does not transfer. Use You → What Lamp and Path holds → Export on the old phone to keep a copy. Your subscription moves with your Apple ID: tap Restore.

Delete my data

Go to You → What Lamp and Path holds and erase everything, or delete the app. We hold no copy.

The scripture or reflection did not fit

You can correct the theme on any Moment and get a new passage. Scripture is quoted from the World English Bible, British Edition. Reflections are written to help you think the passage through. They are not a message from God and not pastoral advice.
